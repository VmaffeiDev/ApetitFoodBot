# Fotos ilustrativas

`pratos.webp` é um atlas de oito fotografias ilustrativas geradas com a ferramenta
integrada ImageGen para a demonstração. Não representa a operação da Apetit,
ingredientes conferidos ou porções prescritas. As fotos nunca determinam os
avisos de alergênicos.

O arquivo foi reduzido para 1024 × 512 e convertido para WebP. O app usa recortes
por CSS; as prévias incorporam a imagem para funcionar sem arquivos auxiliares.

Prompt de geração:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single food photography texture atlas for a Brazilian cafeteria nutrition app; one image laid out as an exact 4-column by 2-row grid of 8 perfectly square cells, all equal size, with no gutters, no labels, no text, no frame, no watermarks. The entire rectangular atlas aspect ratio is 2:1. Every cell is a separate top-down macro studio food photo on a dark charcoal table, warm appetizing natural side light, realistic homemade Brazilian food, matte white ceramic bowls mostly filling their cell. Cell order left to right: TOP ROW cell 1 sliced roast beef in glossy brown gravy; cell 2 two boiled eggs halved showing bright yellow yolks; cell 3 spaghetti with garlic and olive oil, no other sauce; cell 4 fluffy plain white parboiled rice. BOTTOM ROW cell 1 Brazilian black beans in dark broth; cell 2 fresh mixed green lettuce salad; cell 3 a complete round white dinner plate of rice, black beans, sliced roast beef, lettuce, composed neatly in four areas, whole plate visible; cell 4 another view of the same complete dinner plate of rice, black beans, sliced roast beef, lettuce. Food must stay centered within its own cell for use as cropped small app thumbnails. Exact equal grid, photographic realism, overhead view, no cutlery crossing boundaries, no typography, no UI, no people.
